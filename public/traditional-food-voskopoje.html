Traditional food in Voskopojë, cooked the old way

Vila Falo is a family-run restaurant and guesthouse in the mountain village of Voskopojë, 21 km above Korçë — lakror baked on the saç, lamb from the mountains, lake cheese and homemade raki.

Where to eat traditional Albanian food in Voskopojë

If you are looking for real, home-style Albanian mountain cooking near Korçë, Vila Falo is the place locals send you. It is a family kitchen first: recipes passed down in Voskopojë, ingredients from the mountains and the lake, and dishes cooked slowly the traditional way. Guests consistently describe the food as fresh, generous and genuinely homemade — and the welcome as warm.

Dishes worth the drive

A few of the traditional specialities. See the full list, with starters, salads, cheeses and grills, on our menu.

Lakror

The signature of the region — thin layers of pastry with seasonal fillings, baked slowly on the saç over embers. Order ahead for the freshest one.

Oven-roasted lamb

Local lamb slow-roasted until it falls off the bone, with potatoes. Also grilled lamb and kid-goat chops from the charcoal.

Petulla & lake cheese

Golden fried pastries rubbed with garlic and cheese, local lake cheese and baked kaçkavall — the classic mountain table.

A table in the mountains

Eat indoors by the fire or outside in the garden. Live music every Saturday evening, and a calm village setting.

Common questions

Where can you eat traditional Albanian food in Voskopojë?

At Vila Falo, a family-run guesthouse and restaurant in Voskopojë, 21 km from Korçë. The kitchen serves traditional Albanian mountain food — lakror baked on the saç, oven-roasted and grilled lamb, local lake cheese, seasonal vegetables and homemade raki.

Can I visit the restaurant just for lunch or dinner, without staying overnight?

Yes. The restaurant is open to everyone, not only overnight guests. Many people drive up from Korçë just for a meal. We are open daily, roughly 08:00 to 23:00.

What are the signature traditional dishes?

Lakror (a thin layered village pie baked on the saç), oven-roasted lamb and grilled lamb or kid-goat chops, lake cheese and baked kaçkavall, village salad, petulla (fried pastries with cheese and garlic) and homemade raki.

Do I need to book a table?

Walk-ins are welcome. For larger groups, or for slow-cooked dishes that need preparation time (lakror, cornmeal bread, village chicken), it helps to message us ahead on WhatsApp so we can have it ready.

How far is Vila Falo from Korçë?

About 21 km — a 25 to 30 minute drive up to the mountain village of Voskopojë. It is easy to reach from Korçë, and also from Greece and North Macedonia.
