Where to stay in Voskopojë with children

Travelling as a family needs different things — room for everyone, a safe place for children to run, an easy way upstairs, and above all a clean, calm, welcoming home. That is exactly what Vila Falo is built for. It is run by one family, and the thing guests mention most is how clean it is kept and how warmly they are looked after — including help with whatever babies and little ones need.

Common questions

Is Vila Falo good for families with children?

Yes — it is very family- and child-friendly. There are spacious family suites, a safe courtyard and garden where children can play, and a calm, clean, welcoming environment. The family owners help with whatever babies and children need, which is why many families with young children and grandparents choose us.

What room options do you have for families?

A Standard Mountain Room for up to 2 guests (from 5,000 lekë / ~€46), a Family Suite for up to 4 (from 6,000 lekë / ~€55), and a Premium Panorama Suite for up to 5 (from 7,000 lekë / ~€65). All include breakfast, heating, a private bathroom and mountain views.

Is there outdoor space for children?

Yes. There is a courtyard and garden with mountain views where children can play safely, plus the quiet village and mountain trails right outside.

Do you have an elevator?

Yes, Vila Falo has an elevator / lift, so every floor is easy to reach — convenient for grandparents, young children and guests with luggage or a stroller.

Is breakfast included, and is parking free?

Yes to both. A home-cooked breakfast with our own mountain honey is included with every room, and free private parking is available on site.

How do I book and how do I pay?

Book directly on the website or message us on WhatsApp. No online payment or deposit is required — you pay on arrival. We confirm your room quickly on WhatsApp.
